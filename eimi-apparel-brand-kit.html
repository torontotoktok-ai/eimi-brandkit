01 — Brand Foundation

The Core
Identity

Brand Mission

EIMI APPAREL exists to redefine what premium basics mean for the modern generation — crafting garments that speak through silence. Every piece is a deliberate act of restraint: where less becomes the loudest statement, and quality becomes the only embellishment worth having.

Brand Vision

To become the most coveted minimalist streetwear label in Southeast Asia and beyond — a brand synonymous with quiet confidence, effortless cool, and the kind of timeless aesthetic that transcends seasonal trends. EIMI is not a moment. EIMI is a movement.

Brand Values

Intentional Restraint Quiet Confidence Material Integrity Cultural Fluency Timeless Over Trendy Considered Luxury

Brand Personality

EIMI speaks like the most stylish person in a room who never needs to announce it. Reserved yet magnetic. Aware without being arrogant. The brand carries the energy of Harajuku silence — of someone who moves through space with deliberate grace, dressed entirely in shades of shadow and stone.

Brand Voice & Tone

Sparse. Poetic. Precise. EIMI speaks in fragments that carry weight. Copy is never verbose — every word earns its place. Tone ranges from contemplative editorial prose in long-form contexts to single, crystalline lines in product and campaign contexts. Silence is also a brand voice.

Sparse Poetic Measured Authoritative Never Loud

Unique Selling Proposition

EIMI occupies the rare space between Korean quiet fashion and Western premium streetwear — producing considered wardrobe essentials with editorial presence. Where other brands shout, EIMI silences. Where others chase hype, EIMI builds permanence. The garment is the statement.

"Wear less. Say more."

Emotional Branding Strategy

Aspiration Layer

EIMI makes the customer feel they belong to a curated world of taste. Wearing EIMI signals discernment — not wealth. It communicates that you choose well, care about craft, and understand that fashion can be silent poetry.

Identity Layer

The brand becomes an extension of personal identity — calm, considered, and modern. EIMI aligns with people who find beauty in restraint. This identity alignment creates repeat customers who feel the brand "gets" them before they say a word.

Experience Layer

From digital discovery to physical unboxing, every EIMI touchpoint triggers a sensory response. The cool silence of the website. The soft weight of the packaging. The tactile satisfaction of the garment. Every moment is curated luxury.

Layout Philosophy

The primary wordmark uses a high-tracked, ultra-weight sans-serif to command space with geometric confidence. The subordinate "Apparel" descriptor is set at 9-10% of the primary size in condensed light weight — ensuring hierarchy without competition. Minimum clear space: 2× the cap-height of the primary mark on all sides.

Shape Psychology

The four letters E-I-M-I contain a deliberate visual rhythm: open forms followed by closed vertical strokes. This creates a visual "breath" in the mark — expansion and contraction that mirrors the brand's philosophy of restraint. The geometric sans letterforms carry authority without aggression.

Fashion Influence

The wordmark draws from the logotype tradition of Celine (pre-2018), Loro Piana, and Totème — brands that use pure typographic marks without icons or ornament. The secondary serif italic variant nods to Korean luxury brands like Gentle Monster and Ader Error in their editorial applications.

Embroidery Application

For fabric embroidery, use the primary wordmark at minimum 18mm width. The stamp version (EA monogram in a square border) is optimized for chest-patch and sleeve embroidery at 20–30mm. No serifs, gradients, or fine details — all embroidery versions must use solid flat fills with paths no thinner than 1.5mm at final size.

Color Versions

Black on white. White on black. Stone (#8A8580) on off-white — for interior labels, tissue paper watermarks, and muted secondary applications. Avoid color versions unless for a limited campaign. The brand's monochrome discipline is a form of luxury restraint that should never be compromised for trend.

Social Media Icon

The EA monogram in a perfect square — 1:1 ratio, full black background, white letterforms at 72pt Syne ExtraBold, letter-spacing 0.05em. No border radius. No drop shadow. No glow. The severity of the square icon communicates premium fashion seriousness in a 40×40px thumbnail that reads clearly at every scale.

Autumn / Winter — The Architecture of Quiet

Pairing A — Editorial / Luxury

Dressed in the architecture of restraint.

Cormorant Garamond Italic as headline. Jost Light as body. This pairing creates the classic tension of old-world serif luxury against modern humanist sans — the same visual grammar used by Jacquemus, Acne Studios, and COS in editorial contexts. Tracking on the serif: +0.04em. Body line-height: 1.85.

Pairing B — Streetwear / Campaign

VOID SEASON

Syne ExtraBold for the mark. Barlow Condensed for the support text. High tracking. Full caps. This pairing channels the graphic-design precision of Supreme, Palace, and Korean brand Ader Error — strong typographic hierarchy where the headline dominates and everything else defers. Headline tracking: +0.15em. Support: +0.25em, all-caps.

Premium Font Alternatives

For highest-tier applications (packaging, campaign, lookbooks), consider licensed fonts: Neue Haas Grotesk (logo alternative to Syne — the typeface of MUJI and many Korean minimal brands), Canela by Commercial Type (editorial display, used by Ssense and Ganni), or Freight Display for luxury packaging.

Letter Spacing Guide

Logo primary wordmark: +0.22em. Section titles: +0.05em. Body text: 0 (natural). Utility labels: +0.20em. Monogram: +0.05em. The more utilitarian the use, the more generous the tracking — creating the fashion-label visual signature that reads as architectural rather than casual.

05 — Pattern & Graphic Style

Surface
Language

Pattern concepts designed for tissue paper, tote bags, packaging liners, and subtle print applications across the EIMI product range.

Minimal Rule Lines

0.5px hairlines at 24px pitch. Black on white or reversed. Applications: tissue paper interior, box liner, tote bag woven texture reference.

EA Grid System

Architectural grid derived from the letter geometry. Maps 1:1 with the EA monogram proportions. Usage: packaging liner, lookbook grid, website section backgrounds.

Diagonal Grain

45° hairline repeat. Soft grunge texture effect at scale. Applications: shipping mailer texture, sticker background, garment care tag backing.

Micro Dot Field

1px radial dot grid at 18px pitch. Ultra-minimal texture. Use at 50% opacity over white for tissue paper and interior packaging. Embossable pattern at 1:8 scale.

Oversized repeating wordmark at reduced opacity (15–20%). Applications: interior packaging base, branded tissue paper, tote bag all-over print, clothing care card border.

Large ghosted EA at low opacity behind product details. Creates depth without distraction. Applications: luxury paper bag interior, product photography backdrop.

Woven Linen Texture

Seigaiha Wave

06 — Apparel Design Direction

Collection
Architecture

Ten collection concepts that define the EIMI seasonal range — each a distinct visual and material world, all sharing the same foundational aesthetic.

01

VOID — Core Black Series

The essential collection. Heavyweight oversized tees, premium hoodies, and unstructured joggers in obsidian, carbon, and charcoal. Minimal tonal embroidery. No graphics. The garment speaks through cut and weight alone.

02

LINEN SEASON — Summer Neutral

Washed linen and cotton-linen blend pieces. Drop-shoulder silhouettes in paper white, parchment, and warm fog. Garments designed to wrinkle beautifully and feel more personal with each wear.

03

SEOUL GREY — Urban Edit

Asymmetric paneled hoodies, wide-leg technical trousers, and oversized coaches in slate-blue gray tones. The cold-urban aesthetic of Seoul's Hongdae district translated into wearable form.

04

TERROIR — Earth Archive

Natural fiber garments in tobacco, camel, and espresso. Subtle distressed dye effects on heavyweight fleece. The collection references the aesthetics of Japanese boro patchwork and Korean natural dye tradition in a modern streetwear silhouette.

05

MONOLITH — Editorial Capsule

Limited-edition capsule. One garment per silhouette. Pure white or pure black. Oversized jacket, single-seam tee, wide trouser. Campaign-grade photography only. Drop quantity: under 100 units per item.

06

GHOST — Washed Muted Tone

Pre-washed, slightly faded garments that arrive already wearing history. Muted moss, faded slate, washed bone. Each piece is enzyme-washed to soften and create unique tonal variance — no two pieces identical.

07

ARCHIVE — Vintage Reissue

Reimagined vintage athletic silhouettes — coach jackets, crewneck sweatshirts, track pants — rebuilt in premium materials and neutral tones. Vintage-inspired proportions with a modern quiet aesthetic. No retro graphics.

08

DUSK — Soft Winter Fleece

Heavy-weight fleece collection for winter. Boxy silhouettes. Tactile surface in dusty plum, charcoal, and slate. The collection is designed to be worn as outerwear in temperate climates — structured enough to wear over nothing, soft enough to wear as an inner layer.

09

NEGATIVE — White Statement Series

The inverse of VOID. Premium white garments with black detail. Tonal white-on-white embroidery. Clean, clinical, and quietly radical. The anti-color statement. Laundering instructions printed in oversized black type on the inside hem — visible when worn untucked as a design detail.

10

OBJECTS — Accessories Drop

Non-apparel capsule: canvas tote bags, structured caps, minimal canvas pouches, phone sleeves. All in VOID palette. Designed to carry EIMI identity without garments — building brand touchpoints across daily utility objects.

Print & Embroidery Placement Guide

CHT

Chest Left — Minimal Logo

3–4cm embroidered EIMI wordmark at chest-left position. Thread color: matching or 10% darker than garment. No fill stitch — outline only for refined look. Standard placement for all core collection garments.

BCK

Oversized Back Print

Large-format back print using campaign typography or EA monogram pattern at 30–40cm width. For statement pieces only. Print technique: water-based discharge or screen print for a soft, absorbed finish.

SLV

Sleeve Type Treatment

Vertical text running down left or right sleeve: "EIMI APPAREL" in Barlow Condensed, 1.5cm height. Rotated 90°. Applied to hoodies, coaches, and long-sleeve tees. Creates a fashion-editorial visual that references Fear of God and Represent.

NPC

Neckline Interior Label

Woven label at neckline: EIMI wordmark + size + fabric content. Additional printed care label on left side seam. Both carry brand personality — even the care label uses brand typography. No generic label language.

HEM

Hem Drop Text

Small-scale typographic detail on the lower hem edge: season name, collection number, or brand phrase in 8pt Barlow Condensed. Visible when garment is worn untucked. Creates a deliberate "found detail" for the discerning customer.

ALL

All-Over Tonal Print

Reserved for seasonal capsules only. EA monogram or logotype repeat at 30% opacity on matching garment tone. Effect: barely visible pattern that reveals itself in certain light conditions. The print as whisper, not shout.

08 — Social Media Branding

Digital
Presence

EIMI's digital identity follows the same discipline as its garments — every post is curated, every frame deliberate. No clutter. No noise. No compromise.

Instagram

Feed Aesthetic

3×3 grid aesthetic: alternating product-flat, editorial-model, and negative-space-detail frames. Color correction to MONOLITH palette — desaturated, with a cool +5 hue shift and −10 saturation. Consistent 1:1 ratio for grid integrity. No carousel infographics. Every post earns its existence.


Story Templates

Full-bleed black background. White Syne ExtraBold for any text. Only used for: restock alerts, drop countdowns, and behind-the-scenes textile detail shots. No aesthetic-breaking announcement graphics. Brand discipline applies equally to temporary content.

TikTok

Video Aesthetic

Cinematic slow-motion unboxing. Fabric texture close-ups in natural light. GRWM (Get Ready With Me) content by the brand's own aesthetic — no fast-cut, no trending audio for its own sake. Music: lo-fi instrumental Korean or Japanese ambient only. Content tone: calm confidence. Duration: 15–45 seconds.


Viral Content Formula

The "Before and After" outfit transformation (no music, ASMR fabric sounds only). The "This is how we pack your order" — slow, satisfying, premium. The "Why our shirts cost what they cost" — fabric closeups, factory process, transparency. These perform because they answer real audience questions with brand integrity.

Pinterest

Board Strategy

Five boards: (1) EIMI Wardrobe — curated product shots. (2) The EIMI Aesthetic — inspirational imagery matching brand mood. (3) Seoul / Tokyo Reference — city aesthetics the brand draws from. (4) Texture Library — fabric and material close-ups. (5) Architecture of Quiet — minimal spaces and objects that share the brand's visual DNA.


Save-Worthy Content

Outfit formulas (specific product + how to style it). Seasonal edit roundups. Brand mood graphics with campaign lines in Cormorant Garamond italic. These drive purchase-intent traffic from aspirational savers directly to product pages.

Content Pillars — 5-Bucket Strategy

30% Product

Clean product photography, flat-lays, texture close-ups. Always in brand palette. Never on white boxes or colored backgrounds.

25% Editorial

Campaign-quality model photography. Cinematic lighting. Mono or duotone editing. The aspirational pillar.

20% Culture

Seoul/Tokyo aesthetic references. Music, art, design that resonates with the brand world. No direct product.

15% Process

Fabric sourcing, packaging creation, behind-scenes craft. Builds brand trust and transparency narrative.

10% Community

Customer OOTD reposts (curated only — must match aesthetic). No ugly reposted screenshots. Edit first, always.

Lighting Direction

Single natural light source — never ring lights or direct flash. Window light from left or behind-left creates the architectural shadow play the brand requires. No reflectors (allow shadow to exist). For studio shots: large format softbox at 45° camera-left only. The goal is sculptural — treating fabric as architecture, the model as the landscape it inhabits.

Model Direction

Neutral expressions. Looking away from lens in 70% of shots. Hands in pockets or at sides — never posed unnaturally. Models cast for their presence, not conventional beauty. Diverse in every dimension except aesthetic alignment — every person in front of an EIMI camera must embody the brand's quiet-confidence archetype.

Editing Style

Lightroom preset: −15 saturation, +8 contrast, −10 highlights, +15 shadows, +3 cool tint. All EIMI photography exists in a slight desaturated cool world — never vivid, never warm. A consistent edit across all content creates the visual coherence that makes the brand recognizable before the logo is visible. The edit is the signature.

Locations

Architectural spaces. Empty parking structures. Concrete stairwells. Gallery-white rooms. Rooftops at dusk. Quiet café interiors — but shot from the perspective of an observer, not a participant. EIMI garments are photographed in environments that share their visual language: structure, restraint, and considered space.
